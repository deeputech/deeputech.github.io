Operations

Cut range

Removes the selected range. Set Selection Start/End under the player, or drag on the brush bar.

Speed

Factor > 1 speeds up, < 1 slows down. Applied to the selected range, or the whole cast if no range.

Quantize idle

Cap any gap between events to at most this many seconds.

Marker

Drops a chapter marker at the playhead (click the bar to move it). Shows as an amber flag on the bar and as a chapter in players that support markers.
